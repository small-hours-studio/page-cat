Why cats sit in boxes

By a staff writer · 6 min read

Cats love small enclosed spaces. A box offers a warm, safe corner from which a cat can watch the world without being seen, and researchers think the snug fit helps them relax.

Shelters have found that giving new arrivals a hiding box lowers their stress within days. The effect is strongest for cats that are shy around people.

Quick tip

Put a cardboard box near a sunny window. Most cats will find it within the hour.

What the research says

Studies of shelter cats suggest that boxes give them a way to cope with change. Cats with boxes adapted faster than cats without them.

  • Boxes are warm, which cats like.
  • Boxes hide them from predators and people.
  • Boxes make good places to ambush toys.
