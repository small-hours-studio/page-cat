Page Cat privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Page Cat is a browser extension that draws an animated cat on the web pages you visit.

What it collects

Nothing. Page Cat does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data, browsing history, page content, or usage statistics. It makes no network requests of any kind.

What it stores

Your cat settings (size, girth, coat, pattern, activity, and on/off switches) an optional list of sites where you have hidden the cat, and any custom cats you have imported (their name, size and markings). These are saved with Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.sync, so they stay in your browser and, if you use Chrome sync, in your own Google account. The developer never sees them.

What it reads on a page

To decide where the cat can walk, Page Cat reads the size and position of elements on the page you are viewing. To play, it may briefly hide one small image or short word on the page and draw a copy of it, then put the original back exactly as it was. This happens only inside your browser, only while the page is open, and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • storage: saves your cat settings.
  • All sites and scripting: let the cat appear on the page you're viewing and follow you from tab to tab. On each page it only reads where elements are, to find places to walk. You can turn following off, or keep the cat off any site, from the toolbar menu.

Custom cat orders

If you order a custom cat on our order page, you upload photos of your cat, its name, and your email address. The photos are kept by our order service (hosted on Cloudflare) and sent to Anthropic's Claude AI, which makes your cat's design; Anthropic doesn't use them to train its models. The photos are deleted once you approve your cat, or within a few days if you don't finish paying. Your email address is used only for your receipt and your cat's code. Payment is handled by Stripe; we never see your card details. The cat code holds only the cat's name, colors and markings.
